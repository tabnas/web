# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We — contributors and maintainers — pledge to make participation in the tabnas
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments,
publishing others' private information, and other conduct that would reasonably
be considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the
maintainers at **richard@ricebridge.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly.
