Welcome to **tabnas.dev**. tabnas is a polyglot parser engine: you describe a
language once, and get a working parser that builds a small, uniform syntax
tree — the same grammar and the same tree in TypeScript and Go.

This site is where that story comes together:

- **Docs** — every package's tutorial, guide, reference, and concepts, in both
  languages.
- **Playground** — try a grammar in your browser, powered by the real engine.
- **AI agents** — constrain model output to a grammar, parse agent DSLs, and
  read agent-consumable docs.
- **Community** — questions, ideas, and contributions welcome.

tabnas is open source, sponsored by [Voxgig](https://voxgig.com).

The name is Irish — *Tábla na nAistrithe*, "a table of translations." A grammar
is exactly that: a table that turns one form into another. More soon.
