# tabnas for AI agents

<p class="lede">
  Agents live and die by structure. tabnas gives you grammars as a first-class
  tool — to constrain what a model emits, to parse what it produces, and to
  make this documentation legible to the agents reading it.
</p>

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Constrain output" level="h2">
    Define the exact shape you expect back — a command, a query, a config —
    as a grammar, then parse the model's response. Malformed output fails
    loudly at a known position instead of silently corrupting downstream
    state.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Parse agent DSLs" level="h2">
    Tool-call syntaxes, planning notations, and mini-languages are just
    grammars. Describe one once and parse it identically in your TypeScript
    orchestrator and your Go runtime.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Agent-consumable docs" level="h2">
    This site publishes an [`llms.txt`](/llms.txt) index and a full
    [`llms-full.txt`](/llms-full.txt), plus raw-markdown endpoints (e.g.
    `/blog/<slug>.md`), so coding agents can ingest tabnas directly — no
    scraping guesswork.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
